# My Favorite Series: Harry Potter

## About the Series
Harry Potter is a fantasy series written by J.K. Rowling. It follows the adventures of a young wizard named Harry Potter as he studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

## Main Characters
- **Harry Potter** - A brave young wizard.
- **Hermione Granger** - An intelligent and hardworking witch.
- **Ron Weasley** - Harry's loyal best friend.
- **Albus Dumbledore** - The wise headmaster of Hogwarts.

## My Favorite Movie
**Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban**

I like this movie because it has exciting adventures, magic, and interesting mysteries.

## Lessons I Learned
1. Friendship is important.
2. Always be brave.
3. Never give up, even when things get difficult.
